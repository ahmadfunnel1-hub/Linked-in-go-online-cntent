## E1 · Day 2 · Same question, different numbers

**Subject:** Same business. Same question. Different numbers.
**Alt subject:** Why your reports disagree
**Preview:** The mismatch usually starts in one of three places.

Hi {{contact.first_name}},

Ask three teams for "revenue last quarter" and you may get three numbers.

Nobody is wrong. The mismatch usually starts in one of three places:

- **Definitions:** are teams measuring the same thing (before or after returns, which entities)?
- **Timing:** do the reports cover the same period?
- **Sources:** which systems feed each report?

When that happens, meetings start with reconciling numbers instead of making decisions.

That's the problem SAP Business Data Cloud is designed to solve: bringing SAP and non-SAP data together **with its business context**, so teams work from the same definitions.

Try this: pick one KPI and ask two teams how they calculate it.
If the answers differ, it's worth a conversation:

SAP Analytics Cloud, part of SAP Business Data Cloud, gives finance a shared environment for planning and analysis. Assumptions stay connected, and plans update consistently.
