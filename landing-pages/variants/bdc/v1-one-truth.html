The problem

Your business is moving forward. Is your financial planning still looking back?

Revenue expectations shift, costs rise and markets move, and leadership expects finance to have answers. These aren't reporting questions. They're decisions that depend on understanding what could happen next.

What happens if revenue falls below expectations?
How will rising costs affect our margins?
Can we evaluate an investment before committing?
What needs to change in our financial plan?

Sound familiar?

The numbers exist. The planning process gets in the way.

Evaluating a new scenario can still mean hours of manual updates, several versions of one model and repeated checks. By the time the forecast reaches leadership, the assumptions may already have changed.

Budgets go out of date fast

Time spent maintaining spreadsheets

"What if" takes too long

Disconnected assumptions

Looking back, not ahead

The real problem isn't a lack of data

It's the gap between the information you have and the decisions your business needs to make.

Better reporting alone won't close that gap. More dashboards and more spreadsheets don't create a repeatable planning process. You need a way to use information to evaluate decisions.

FAQ

Common questions

Is SAP Analytics Cloud only for reporting and dashboards?

No. SAP Analytics Cloud supports analytics and planning, including financial planning, scenario modeling and forecasting. The capabilities you use depend on your requirements and the solution design. This offer focuses on planning and decision support.

We already plan in Excel. Why consider SAC?

Excel remains a useful finance tool. But as models grow more complex and the need for collaboration, scenario comparison and changing assumptions increases, spreadsheet-heavy processes get harder to maintain. The aim is to find where your current approach creates limits, and whether SAC addresses them.

Will our finance team need technical expertise?

That depends on how the solution is designed and each user's tasks. Practical, role-based enablement is part of the delivery approach.

Can SAP Analytics Cloud work with our existing systems?

SAP Analytics Cloud offers integration capabilities. The right approach depends on your applications, data sources and architecture, and is reviewed during assessment and design.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on scope: planning-model complexity, integrations, processes, testing and user readiness. We agree a realistic delivery plan with your stakeholders.

What happens after go-live?

Planning requirements keep evolving. The offer includes options for ongoing managed services, support and optimization, subject to the agreed engagement scope.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is tailored to scope, implementation complexity, enablement needs and ongoing services. Requirements and the commercial approach are defined together with Intrasoft.

What happens after I download the e-book?

You get the e-book straight away. If you opted in, Intrasoft may follow up with related insights and an invitation to discuss your planning challenges, with no obligation.

How do we know whether it's working?

Success measures are agreed before implementation begins, such as planning-process efficiency, user adoption and scenario-analysis capability. Specific business or financial results depend on your context.
