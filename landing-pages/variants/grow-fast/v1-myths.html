FAQ

Common questions

What is SAP GROW Fast?

An accelerated way to start with SAP Cloud ERP (public edition). It uses SAP preconfigured best practices and a predefined implementation scope, delivered by an SAP partner such as Intrasoft.

How is the price structured?

There are two parts: the SAP Cloud ERP subscription and Intrasoft's fixed-price GROW Fast service for the predefined scope. Both are agreed upfront. The fixed price covers the agreed scope only. Additional scope, integrations or change requests are quoted separately.

What's included in the starting scope?

The predefined package covers core finance, supply chain and HR processes. We confirm the exact processes in scope for your business during the first conversation.

How long does implementation take?

The target is about four months to go-live. The actual timeline depends on scope, data readiness and your team's availability.

Who runs and supports the system after go-live?

SAP runs the cloud system, including infrastructure, updates, security and maintenance. Intrasoft provides user training and ongoing support.

Our data isn't ready. Can we still start?

Data preparation is part of the plan. Data responsibilities are agreed upfront, and readiness is checked before go-live.

Will the move disrupt our operations?

The transition is planned and tested in advance, with clear responsibilities and go-live preparation, so the switch happens in a controlled way.

Will it fit our manufacturing processes?

We walk you through the relevant standard processes and identify any fit gaps, or requirements outside the package scope, before you commit.

Our team is used to the current systems. Will they adapt?

User training is part of the package, and Intrasoft keeps supporting your team after go-live.

Is this right for us?

GROW Fast suits growing mid-size companies, especially manufacturers, that have outgrown spreadsheets, disconnected systems or a legacy ERP. It's less suited to companies that need extensive bespoke development or a scope well outside the predefined package.
